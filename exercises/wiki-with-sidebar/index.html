Super Mario

History

In 1980, Nintendo released Radar Scope, their first arcade video game,[24] which was a commercial failure.[25][26]: 94,103 Nintendo president Hiroshi Yamauchi appointed Shigeru Miyamoto to repurpose unused Radar Scope arcade cabinets into a new game.[24][25] While Miyamoto wanted the game to star Popeye characters,[27] negotiations between Nintendo and Popeye owner King Features fell through, and the game required reworking.[26]:47 Three new characters were created for the game instead: Jumpman, Lady, and Donkey Kong.[27][28] Titled Donkey Kong, the game was released in arcades in 1981.[29] Lady was later renamed to Pauline,[30] and Jumpman was renamed to Mario.[26]:109 Miyamoto created Mario's brother Luigi using palette swap of Mario's sprite.[25] Mario and Luigi starred together in the 1983 arcade game Mario Bros.[31] The gameplay of Donkey Kong and Mario Bros., centered around the act of jumping to dodge obstacles, caused the games to be considered landmark titles in the platformer genre of games.[32][25]
